Straight answers.

How much does a restaurant website cost?

Between $600 and $1,200 as a one-time project, depending on how many pages you need and how much writing is involved. Most local restaurants land around $750. The site is delivered within 48 to 72 hours of payment and works properly on phones.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Monthly work is month to month. Cancel whenever you want with a week of notice. If the work isn't earning its keep you shouldn't be locked into it.

How much time does this take me?

About thirty seconds a week. Every Monday you get a text asking what's happening — a special, an event, or nothing at all. That's the whole ask. Everything else is handled, and nothing gets published without you seeing it first.

What if I don't have good photos?

We work with what's already on your Instagram and Google listing. If you want new photos, a phone camera and decent daylight covers most of it — we'll tell you exactly what to shoot and when the light in your space is best.

Which parts of Oahu do you work with?

All of it — Kaneohe, Kailua, Honolulu, Haleiwa, Waimanalo, Aiea, Kapolei, Waianae. Oahu businesses only. Staying local is the point.

Is the free week actually free?

Yes. Five Instagram posts and one email written for your restaurant, no cost and no commitment. You keep the content either way. If you like it we talk about monthly. If not, no hard feelings.
