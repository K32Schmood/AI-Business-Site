Questions

Straight answers.

How much does a restaurant website cost in Hawaii?

Between $600 and $1,200 as a one-time project, depending on how many pages and how much copywriting is needed. Most local restaurants land around $750. The site is delivered within 48 to 72 hours and works properly on phones.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Monthly work is month to month. Cancel whenever you want with one week of notice. If the work isn't earning its keep you shouldn't be locked into it.

How much time does this take on my end?

About thirty seconds a week. Every Monday you get a text asking what's happening — a special, an event, or nothing at all. That's the whole ask. Everything else is handled, and nothing gets posted without you seeing it first.

What if I don't have good photos?

We work with what's already on your Instagram and Google listing. If you need new photos, a phone camera and decent daylight covers most of it — we'll tell you exactly what to shoot and when the light in your space is best.

Which parts of Oahu do you work with?

All of Oahu — Kaneohe, Kailua, Honolulu, Haleiwa, Waimanalo, Aiea, Kapolei, Waianae. Oahu businesses only. Staying local is the point.

Is the free week actually free?

Yes. Five Instagram posts and one email written for your restaurant, no cost and no commitment. You keep the content whether or not you continue. If you like it we talk monthly. If not, no hard feelings.
